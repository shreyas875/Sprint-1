About Smart Event Finder

Smart Event Finder is a full-stack platform that helps people discover concerts, workshops, conferences, sports, meetups and more — all in one place. Built with vanilla JavaScript, Node.js, Express and MySQL, it demonstrates a complete real-world event discovery and registration workflow.

What you can do

  • Search and filter events by category, city, date, price and type
  • Find events near you using your device location
  • Get personalized recommendations based on your interests
  • Bookmark events and register with real-time seat tracking
  • Manage your registrations and saved events from your dashboard
